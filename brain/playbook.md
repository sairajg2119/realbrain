# RealBrain playbook (rebuilt draft)

> Rebuilt from the project brief, NOT from the original 485-reel analysis. Treat the patterns as a starting point. Replace with the original playbook when it is found.

## Winning formats for developers
1. Developer math - walk through the numbers of a deal or a home (what you pay, what you get).
2. Construction spectacle - time-lapse or close-up of the build, with one surprising fact.
3. Corridor transformation tour - "this road / this area, then and now" and what is coming.
4. Founder on camera - the person behind the project says one honest thing.
5. Narrated walkthrough - a voice guides you through the home, room by room.
6. One standout feature - a single rare feature gets the whole reel.
7. Market proof - a fact about demand, supply or price movement that makes the project's case.
8. Fast visual tour + story caption - quick cuts, the story sits in the caption.
9. Just-sold story - a real result (only if the brochure states it).

## Hook types (templates)
- **bold money number**: "<Number from the brochure> is what it costs to ..."
- **pattern-interrupt question**: "Why would anyone pick a <config> in <locality>?"
- **place + transformation**: "<Locality> looked like this a few years ago. Look at it now."
- **curiosity + superlative**: "The one thing in <locality> nobody tells you about."
- **rare feature**: "A <feature> inside a <config>. Yes, really."
- **audience call-out**: "If you are a <buyer type> looking in <locality>, watch this."
- **question + analogy**: "Is a home like a <analogy>? Here is why it might be."
- **confession**: "We almost did not include this in the project. Here is why we did."
- **poetic contrast**: "Quiet mornings. A short drive from the city's noise."

## CTAs
- Default: comment keyword, then DM. Example: "Comment HOME and we will send the floor plans."
- Lifestyle reels may end with share bait: "Send this to someone who wants a home like this."
- Never a phone number only.

## The 10 rules every script must pass
1. The hook is one of the hook types above and lands in under 3 seconds.
2. The first line never starts with the project name or a spec.
3. At least 3 specific facts from the brochure.
4. At least 1 locality fact from the verified locality list (if the locality has any).
5. One clear buyer type per script.
6. A new fact or scene every 3 to 5 seconds.
7. Ends with a comment-keyword CTA (or share bait for lifestyle reels).
8. The RERA registration number is in the caption.
9. Spoken words fit the length: about 2.5 words per second.
10. A one-line "why this works" citing a real reel from the teardowns.

## Style
Plain, warm words. Short sentences. Say what the home gives the buyer, not what the brochure says about itself. Never promise returns, dates or prices that are not in the brochure.
