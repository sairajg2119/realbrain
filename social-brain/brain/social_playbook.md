## Failure patterns checked against the data

- **Very short or empty caption**: more common among underperformers (lift 1.54, 9/158 underperformers; 2 breakouts still have it). Rule: Put the story and CTA keyword in the caption.
- **Spec-line or project-name opening**: no clear association (lift 1.06, 25/158 underperformers; 7 breakouts still have it). Rule: Never open on the project name, BHK or area spec. Open on a person, a number with a story, or a question.
- **Plain listing description as the hook**: no clear association (lift 0.98, 13/158 underperformers; 6 breakouts still have it). Rule: A listing fact is a supporting beat, not the hook.
- **No visible CTA**: no clear association (lift 0.97, 98/158 underperformers; 66 breakouts still have it). Rule: Add exactly one CTA to every lead-focused reel.
- **Long runtime (90s or more) without a strong hook**: no clear association (lift 0.87, 13/158 underperformers; 13 breakouts still have it). Rule: Keep under 60 seconds unless the hook is a number, interruption or call-out.
- **Presenting / announcement opening**: not associated with underperformance (lift 0.51, 5/158 underperformers; 8 breakouts still have it). Rule: Do not start with 'Presenting', 'Introducing' or 'New launch'.
- **Phone number as the only CTA**: not associated with underperformance (lift 0.21, 1/158 underperformers; 5 breakouts still have it). Rule: Pair any phone number with a comment-keyword or save CTA.

## Rules

- R1: Open on a hook type, never the project name, a spec line or 'Presenting'. (evidence-checked)
- R2: Use one comment-keyword CTA per lead-focused reel. (evidence-checked; comments are not qualified leads)
- R3: Put the specific number or fact in the first line, then add a new fact every 3 to 5 seconds. (carried from project playbook v1; transcripts too few to verify here)
- R4: Prefer a person on camera over brand-only visuals. (carried from project playbook v1; presenter presence not measurable without video)

## Limitations

- Mostly US pages. Bengaluru is covered by 2 benchmark pages, so findings are not proven for Indian developers.
- One-day snapshot. No metric history yet; each re-run should add a snapshot.
- Rule-based classification of hooks, formats and CTAs from caption and transcript. No video, audio or on-screen text was analysed (visual coverage 0%).
- LLM teardowns cover 486 of 487 reels; the rest use rule-based labels, which are coarser.
- Only 41 of 487 reels have a transcript, so hook type for the rest comes from the caption's first line.
- Likes are hidden (-1) on many reels and treated as unavailable, never zero. Shares, saves and follower counts at collection time are not in the dataset.
- Follower counts, market and category labels come from project notes, not from the scrape.
- Possible paid boosts are invisible in the data. Breakout reels may be boosted.
- Associations only. Nothing here shows that a hook or format caused the views.
